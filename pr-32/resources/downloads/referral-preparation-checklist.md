# Referral Preparation Checklist

Use this checklist before initiating a cross-lens referral.

## Referral context
- [ ] Source organization identified
- [ ] Intended recipient identified
- [ ] Referenced RFx, Resource, Capability, organization, or Intelligence context identified
- [ ] Reason the referral may be useful stated clearly

## Information quality
- [ ] Relevant facts separated from assumptions
- [ ] Sensitive / restricted information excluded unless the governed workflow permits it
- [ ] Expected next step stated

## Policy and terms
- [ ] Recipient referral policy reviewed when available
- [ ] Referral fee / terms reviewed when applicable
- [ ] Parties understand that referral acceptance and downstream outcomes are separate states

## Tracking
- [ ] Sender can identify the referral after submission
- [ ] Status can be reviewed through Referrals Management

This checklist does not create, price, accept, settle, or complete a referral. Those states belong to the authenticated cross-lens referral workflow.
