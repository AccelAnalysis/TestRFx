# Teaming / Partner Checklist

Use this checklist when a known RFx, delivery, capability, or resource gap may require another organization.

## Need
- [ ] The gap is clearly described
- [ ] The requirement / outcome connected to the gap is identified
- [ ] Geography and timing are known
- [ ] Mandatory qualifications are separated from preferences

## Partner criteria
- [ ] Required capability is defined
- [ ] Evidence / credentials needed are defined
- [ ] Capacity / availability needs are defined
- [ ] Service geography fit is defined

## Collaboration readiness
- [ ] Prime / subcontract / supplier role is understood when applicable
- [ ] Workshare expectations are documented
- [ ] Information that can be shared is identified
- [ ] Confidential or restricted information is excluded from the public search criteria

## Decision
- [ ] Candidate organizations compared against the same criteria
- [ ] Open questions documented
- [ ] Next step selected: contact, teaming workflow, referral, or no action

This checklist prepares a search or collaboration decision. It does not create a teaming agreement or referral by itself.
