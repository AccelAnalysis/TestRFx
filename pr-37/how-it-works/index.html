How RFxchange works

One Exchange. Four lenses. One organization identity.

RFxchange establishes an organization once, then lets participants discover and act through RFx, Resources, Intelligence, and Capabilities without leaving the shared Exchange environment.

← Back to the marketing page

1. Establish your organization

Create an account, select or create the organization you represent, establish geography, and complete the organization profile needed to enter the Exchange.

  • Account and organization context
  • Geography and operating area
  • Organization profile and capability enrichment

2. Describe capability in a common language

Capability enrichment connects the organization profile to AMACS so businesses can be discovered by what they can do, not only by a broad industry label.

  • Structured capability data
  • AMACS-aligned categories
  • Evidence-ready capability records

3. Change lenses, not applications

The authenticated Exchange keeps the same map, search, results drawer, cards, and detail behavior while the active lens changes what the participant is looking for.

  • RFx opportunities
  • Resources and providers
  • Market intelligence
  • Organization capabilities

4. Act from context

Governed action positions surface the next appropriate step for the selected record. Workflows become available progressively without changing the operating chassis.

  • Watch, share, save, and follow
  • Respond and team where available
  • Refer across lenses
  • Return to the exact prior Exchange state
